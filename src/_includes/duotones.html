{# Duotone filters, used from CSS with filter: url(#duotone-…). Each one turns a photo grey, then maps black to a dark colour and white to a light colour, with every grey spread evenly between the two. The SVG itself takes no space. Red and yellow use three values per channel (dark, full colour, light): a strong red or yellow can't also be light, so a middle stop keeps the contrast (#193).
